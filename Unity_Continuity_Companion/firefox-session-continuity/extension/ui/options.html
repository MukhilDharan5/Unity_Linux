Forms: explicit approval

Off for every origin until you approve it. Passwords, payment fields, and fields identified as sensitive are excluded. Detection cannot identify every secret. Do not approve sites where you enter confidential information.

    Edits and populated fields are preserved. No forms are submitted. Framework-controlled fields may need manual input.
